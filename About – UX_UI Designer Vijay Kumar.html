About me

Vijay Kumar

Senior Product Designer (UX/UI)

Hi, I’m Vijay Kumar, a Senior Product Designer (UI/UX) with over 10 years of experience crafting meaningful digital experiences for web and mobile platforms. I specialize in transforming complex business problems into intuitive, user-centric designs through a strategic blend of UX research, wireframing, prototyping, and visual storytelling.

Throughout my career, I’ve collaborated with startups, enterprises, and global brands like Ticketmaster, bringing ideas to life through thoughtful design and clean, scalable interfaces. Whether it’s creating design systems, conducting usability tests, or coding responsive frontends, I bring both creativity and precision to every project.

I’m passionate about merging aesthetics with usability, and I enjoy building products that not only look great but work beautifully.

When I’m not designing, you’ll find me sketching, reading, or exploring new ideas that push the boundaries of user experience.
