My design process

A strategic, user-centered & agile approach.

When tackling any product or platform, I don’t just “design screens.” I approach each project with a clear strategy rooted in Design Thinking, User-Centered Design, and Agile Methodologies — creating sustainable, scalable, and intuitive solutions that work for users and business goals.

See what it looks like when I apply this process (Figma demo) →

Phase 01

Discover

Steps 1–4

Where this shows up in my work: multi-country surveys with 4,000+ respondents before redesigning the Ticketmaster Event Dashboard; interviews with drivers, admins, and transport leads across WIOT360.

1

Define Objectives

Before jumping into design, I always start by aligning with stakeholders and understanding the core business goals and success metrics.

Key Tools: Stakeholder Interviews, Brief Analysis

2

Understanding the Problem

I dig deep to fully understand what problem we’re solving — both from the business and user perspective.

3

Audience Discovery & Market Research

To uncover real user needs, I gather both qualitative and quantitative data:

  • Analyze market trends and industry benchmarks
  • Interview users, observe behaviors, and gather feedback
  • Define key findings and insights

Key Tools: Google Trends, Surveys, Hotjar, User Interviews, Feedback Loops

4

Quantitative Insights

Using tools like Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Funnel Analysis, I identify user drop-off points, bounce rates, and behavioral patterns.

Artifact: Funnel Drop-Off Graph from Analytics

Phase 02

Define

Steps 5–9

Where this decided the work: RICE scoring set the redesign order for the Equus HUB and storefronts; personas from real interviews shaped the Sunday Lawn Care onboarding quiz.

5

User Personas

I build personas to keep user needs front and center. These are based on real feedback and data — not guesswork.

Includes: Demographics, Needs & Goals, Pain Points, Motivations

6

Empathy Mapping

I map out users’ thoughts, feelings, behaviors, and challenges to visualize the full emotional journey and uncover hidden friction points.

Artifact: Empathy Map

7

Storyboarding & Scenario Analysis

I use storyboards to simulate real user scenarios and understand context, environment, and sequence of interactions.

Artifact: Storyboard

8

Competitor Analysis

I study competitors to learn what they do well — and more importantly, where they fall short. This helps identify unique opportunities and UX gaps.

9

Ideation & Solution Mapping

Based on insights gathered, I brainstorm unique design solutions that offer real, measurable value to users and stakeholders.

This includes: Value Differentiators, Feature Prioritization (RICE Analysis), Core Problem Areas to Address

Phase 03

Design

Steps 10–12

Where the system mattered more than the screens: one component library across 21 WIOT360 services; one shared design language across all 8 Equus platforms.

10

User Flows & Journey Mapping

I map out the complete journey across touchpoints and build flowcharts to ensure seamless navigation and optimal task completion.

Artifact: Flowchart / User Journey

11

Wireframes & Prototyping

I begin with low-fidelity wireframes to quickly test structure and layout logic. Once validated, I move to interactive mid/high-fidelity prototypes.

Artifacts: Low-Fi Wireframes, Clickable Prototype in Figma

12

UI Design & Usability Testing

I create pixel-perfect high-fidelity designs focused on clarity, accessibility, and brand identity. I also run moderated or unmoderated usability tests to validate interaction patterns and gather final feedback.

Key Tools: Figma, Maze, Userberry, Usertesting.com

Phase 04

Deliver

Steps 13–14

Where handoff was the job, not an afterthought: converting Bitclu designs into AngularJS-ready components and supporting QA through launch; Zeplin, Notion, and Loom walkthroughs for Equus developers.

13

Developer Handoff & Implementation Support

I work closely with developers to ensure seamless handoff — providing clear documentation, annotations, and real-time Slack/Zoom syncs. I actively monitor builds, validate front-end implementation, and help resolve any design gaps.

14

Post-Launch Testing & Continuous Support

Once live, I conduct A/B testing, heatmap reviews, and user feedback collection to validate success. I also offer ongoing design support and improvement cycles.

My guiding methodologies

Design Thinking: Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test

User-Centered Design (UCD): Everything I do revolves around real user data

Agile Workflow: Weekly sprints, stand-ups, backlog grooming, and iterative design cycles

I have often been the only designer in the room, so working closely with developers, engineers, product owners, and stakeholders has never been optional for me — it is how the work actually got shipped. Whether I’m designing an internal dashboard used by Fortune 500 companies or a public-facing eCommerce platform, I apply this process to build thoughtful, high-performing user experiences backed by research, empathy, and measurable outcomes.
